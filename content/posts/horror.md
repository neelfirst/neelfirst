knowledge arises from perception. perception is conditioned. conditioning is adaptive.

knowledge is a local interface, not transparent access, to reality.

imperial power is the attempt to scale a local body of knowledge until it appears universal.

however there is no guarantee that what exists beyond is harmonious, intelligible, or meaningful.

cosmic horror resets the scale.
