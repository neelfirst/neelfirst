human beings inhabit symbolic and embodied structures that condition perception. these structures harden into clichés, models, and archetypes.

they do not merely conceal reality; they construct a humanly inhabitable portion of it. their failures to represent reality reveal both their contingency and their necessity.

to notice conditioning is not necessarily to approach reality more closely, but to discover the limits of the world our conditioning allows us to perceive.
